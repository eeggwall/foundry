# Castle Foundry

Enfilade Center for Recreational Mathematics at Hellmouth Community College

Associated faculty:
- Ch4zm of Hellmouth (@ch4zm)
- Engelbert Eggwall (@eeggwall)

Various divisions:
- *castle representations (R)* division focuses on Dyck-word encodings, tree encodings, matrix encodings, and their equivalences
- *castle enumeration (E)* division studies new methods of enumerating castles
- *castle numbers (N)* division studies the various numbers of all shapes and sizes that come up during castle study, including ways of handling the more difficult ones
- *castle zoo (Z)* division maintains the Enfilade Castle Zoo, an online gallery of specimens
