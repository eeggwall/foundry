CASTLE FOUNDRY
==============
Enfilade Center for Recreational Mathematics at Hellmouth Community College.

Associated faculty:
  - Ch4zm of Hellmouth     (@ch4zm)
  - Engelbert Eggwall      (@eeggwall)

Divisions:
  (R) castle representations  ... dyck words, tree encodings, matrices
  (E) castle enumeration      ... new counting methods
  (N) castle numbers          ... the numbers that arise; the hard ones
  (Z) castle zoo              ... online gallery of specimens

CONTROLS
========
Designer   :: click grid cells to raise / lower tower heights.
              buttons generate random, tallest, widest, or symmetric castles.
              feature toggles filter random generation.
Inspector  :: paste a URD-word (e.g. UUURDURDDD) to view a fact sheet.
Zoo        :: click any specimen to inspect it.

KEYBOARD
========
  G   toggle grid
  A   toggle ascii dump
